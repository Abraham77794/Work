About Us

ISBAT University Student ICT Training Centre is dedicated to providing students with practical Information and Communication Technology skills. The centre offers different training programmes designed to prepare students for the modern technology industry.

Our aim is to equip students with knowledge and practical experience in web development, computer networking, database management and cybersecurity. We provide a learning environment that encourages creativity, innovation and professional development.
